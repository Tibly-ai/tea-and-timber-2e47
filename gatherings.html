Intimate Capacity

Comfortably accommodates up to 24 seated guests for workshops, private dining, or acoustic evening circles.

Our Gathering Philosophy

A seamless transition from workspace to celebration.

We maintain a live wood workshop and active botanical greenhouse by day. When you gather here, you step into a space filled with honest labor, the scent of fresh pine shavings, and brewing herbal infusions.

01

Tailored Curation

We configure our long-leaf pine community tables to suit your group. Whether you need a central banquette for dining, broad benches for timber work, or a clear open floor for acoustic circles.

  • Live Edge Oak Tables
  • Custom Ceramic Tableware
02

Infusion Service

Every gathering includes a dedicated tea steward pouring from our collection of single-origin wild leaves and hand-blended greenhouse botanicals, served in custom wood-fired clay teaware.

  • Infinite Heirloom Steeping
  • Botanical Tasting Profiles
03

Natural Ambience

The sound of trickling water, hundreds of green leaves purifying the air, and soft linen curtains catching the wind. We provide natural acoustic speakers and dimmable warm brass lighting.

  • Cedar Oil Diffusion
  • Dimmable Filament Bulbs
