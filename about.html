The Hands Behind the Craft

Where slow-cured timber meets wild-grown tea leaves.

Founded by a self-taught joiner and a botanist, Tea & Timber was born out of a desire to create a refuge from the frantic pace of modern life. We restored an old greenhouse, lined the tables with white oak offcuts, and invited master tea producers to share their harvests.

Every beam in our space is planed by hand, and every single leaf we brew is sourced directly from small family farms in Uji, Yunnan, and the Oregon hills. We do not rush the drying of our wood, nor the steeping of our bowls.

Our Way of Working

Honoring the natural growth, respecting the raw material.

We rely on methods that protect the integrity of the soil and the wood. No fast kiln-drying, no synthetic pesticide boosters, no compromises.

01

Timber Salvage & Seasoning

We rescue logs from local arborists and historic barn removals around the Pacific Northwest. The lumber is stacked, stickered, and air-dried for up to three years to ensure structural stability and rich, uncompromised grain character.

02

Single-Origin Direct Sourcing

Our tea curation is built on trust with generational growers. By purchasing whole loose leaves during spring and autumn harvests, we support sustainable farming families directly while securing pure, unblended cultivars.

03

The Shared Greenhouse Ritual

Every cup we pour and shelf we build is designed to invite stillness. Our space is kept warm by sun-dappled glass panels, fostering a sensory environment where people, green life, and timber craft exist in harmony.

There is a rare stillness inside Tea & Timber. Sitting at those hand-jointed tables surrounded by jasmine vines, watching the steam rise from a clay bowl, feels like finding an ancient sanctuary right in the middle of our neighborhood.
Eleanor Vance Neighborhood Regular & Botanical Collector
