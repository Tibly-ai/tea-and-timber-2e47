The Tea & Timber Ethos

Where raw timber meets the warmth of a freshly steeped cup.

At Tea & Timber, we believe in materials that get better with age. Our tables are made from cedar trees harvested from our own forest plots, dried and planed right next to our greenhouse. Our Process

How we spend our seasons.

We do not rush. Whether we are curing cedar logs in the mountain breeze or dialing in the water temperature for an experimental batch of oolong, everything happens at its own rhythm.

01

Sourcing with Integrity

Our teas are sourced on small farms with high biodiversity. Our lumber is harvested locally from managed, sustainable woodlots or salvaged from old barn structures across Oregon.

02

Seasoning & Planing

Timber must cure. We store our cut lumber for up to eighteen months inside our dry timber barn. It is then hand-planed and oiled with cold-pressed botanical seeds to highlight the raw, fragrant grain.

03

Steeping to Perfection

Water temperature is tuned down to the single degree to prevent burning delicate white and green teas. We brew in small clay pots to let the tea expand, creating a deep, resonant brew.
