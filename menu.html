Tea, Crumb & Seasoned Timber.

We treat woodcraft and tea infusion as twin rituals. Our botanical selections are harvested from single estates, our grain is milled locally, and every timber spoon or board on our shelves was carved on-site from fallen local cedar, cherry, and oak.
